# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Title 

**👥 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard**
An interactive data analytics project designed to analyze customer churn, understand customer behavior, identify churn patterns, and support customer retention decisions using **Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI**.

---

#  Purpose

The Customer Churn Analysis project analyzes customer demographics, tenure, contract types, payment methods, monthly charges, and service information to understand why customers leave.

The project transforms raw customer data into meaningful insights through **data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL analysis, and Power BI visualization**.

---

#  Tech Stack

* 📊 **Excel** – Data preparation and initial analysis
* 🐍 **Python** – Data cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis
* 🗄️ **SQL / MySQL** – Business analysis and querying
* 📈 **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and visualization
* 🐼 **Pandas** – Data manipulation
* 📊 **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization
  

---

#  Data Source

The dataset contains customer-level information including:

* Customer ID
* Gender
* Senior Citizen
* Partner & Dependents
* Tenure
* Contract
* Monthly Charges
* Total Charges
* Internet Service
* Payment Method
* Churn

---


---

## • Goal of the Project

* Analyze overall customer churn.
* Identify major churn patterns.
* Understand customer behavior.
* Compare churn across contracts and payment methods.
* Analyze tenure and monthly charges.
* Identify customer segments requiring retention efforts.
* Provide data-driven business recommendations.

---

## • Key Analysis

### 📄 Contract Analysis

Analyzes churn across different contract types to identify high-churn customer groups.

### ⏳ Tenure Analysis

Studies the relationship between customer tenure and churn.

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

Compares churn across different payment methods.

### 💰 Monthly Charges Analysis

Analyzes whether customers with different monthly charges show different churn behavior.

### 👥 Customer Analysis

Examines churn based on gender, senior citizen status, partner, and dependents.

---

## • Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard provides:

* Total Customers
* Churned Customers
* Retained Customers
* Churn Rate
* Churn by Contract
* Churn by Tenure
* Churn by Payment Method
* Churn by Monthly Charges
* Customer Demographics

---

#  Business Impact & Insights

### 🎯 Customer Retention

Identify high-churn customer groups and develop targeted retention strategies.

### 📄 Contract Optimization

Encourage customers toward longer-term contracts.

### ⏳ New Customer Retention

Focus on customers with shorter tenure through better onboarding and engagement.

### 💰 Pricing Analysis

Monitor customers with higher monthly charges and provide suitable offers where required.

### 📊 Data-Driven Decisions

Use the Power BI dashboard to monitor churn and support customer retention planning.
